# Job Satisfaction

## DORA Research Context

Early DORA analysis found that job satisfaction predicts organizational performance. Employees with proper tools, meaningful work, and valued judgment produce better work, leading to higher delivery and organizational performance. Employees rate meaningful work as highly as salary.

## What It Is

Job satisfaction predicts organizational performance — engaged employees doing meaningful, challenging work drive business value through a virtuous circle of continuous improvement.

## Problem Solved

- High turnover; difficulty retaining talent
- Disengagement; employees going through the motions
- Tools and resources don't match what people need to do their jobs
- Improvement work is always deferred for "real work"

## Key Practices

1. **Give employees the tools and resources needed:** To do their work — teams that can choose tools do better at CD.
2. **Give employees meaningful work:** That leverages their expertise.
3. **Make time and resources available for improvement work:** Creating change takes time.
4. **Build automation into the delivery process:** Improving process is itself a skill.
5. **Allow practitioner tool choice:** No one knows better than practitioners what they need.

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why It Fails | What To Do Instead |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Not giving people needed tools | Frustration, slower delivery, burnout | Provide tools and autonomy to choose |
| Not giving meaningful work | Disengagement; work feels pointless | Align work with expertise and purpose |
| No time for improvement | Process never gets better; frustration grows | Allocate dedicated improvement time |

## How to Measure

Job satisfaction is perceptual — you must survey. If you worry about inaccurate answers, that itself signals a problem.

## Coaching Patterns

1. **Survey anonymously:** To establish a baseline of satisfaction and tool adequacy.
2. **Give teams tool autonomy:** Let them choose tools within a baseline, with exceptions.
3. **Make meaningful work visible:** Connect daily tasks to customer outcomes.

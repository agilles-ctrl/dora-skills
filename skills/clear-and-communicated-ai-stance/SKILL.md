# Clear and Communicated AI Stance

## DORA Research Context

DORA found a clear and communicated AI stance amplifies AI's positive influence on individual effectiveness and organizational performance. It turns AI's neutral effect on friction into a beneficial decrease. Ambiguity creates risk both from conservative avoidance and from unchecked shadow AI. This capability is part of the [AI model](/ai/#explore-the-model).

## What It Is

A comprehensible, widely socialized framework that tells developers how they are expected to use AI, which tools they can use, and how the organization supports them. It removes ambiguity, creating psychological safety for experimentation while establishing guardrails.

## Problem Solved

- Developers are afraid to use AI tools (fear of violating unknown policies)
- "Shadow AI" — developers use unvetted tools because there's no guidance
- AI policy is unclear, outdated, or written by one department without engineering input
- Organization swings between prohibiting everything and allowing everything

## Key Practices

1. **Secure executive sponsorship:** Leadership must define and champion the AI mission.
2. **Form a cross-functional working group:** Engineering, legal, security, IT, product leadership.
3. **Adopt a three-bucket framework:** Prohibited (high-risk, never), Permitted with guardrails, Allowed (low-risk, encouraged).
4. **Publish as a living document:** Central, searchable hub with approved tools, policy, and evolving FAQ.
5. **Socialize through town halls and team meetings:** Establish feedback loops for questions.
6. **Measure four perceptions:** Expectation, Support, Permission, Applicability.

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why It Fails | What To Do Instead |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| "One-time" policy | AI technology changes monthly; static policy becomes irrelevant | Review and update regularly |
| "Whiplash" effect | Changing policy too frequently without adaptation time | Give teams time to adjust between updates |
| Myopic authorship | Legal or Security dictates without Engineering | Cross-functional authorship essential |

## How to Measure

- **Survey:** Clarity (which tools allowed?), support (for experimentation?), expectation (is AI mandatory?), applicability (to my role?)
- **Policy engagement:** Page views and dwell time on AI policy docs
- **Clarification volume:** Number of questions in public channels about tool approval

## Coaching Patterns

1. **Establish the three-bucket framework first:** It's simple, clear, and covers the spectrum.
2. **Publish as a living doc:** Review quarterly; update as tools and risks evolve.
3. **Run a "lunch and learn":** Socialize the policy; let teams ask questions directly.

# Team Experimentation

## DORA Research Context

DORA research identifies three components: ability to work on new ideas without permission, ability to change specs during development, and ability to change stories without external approval. These drive both software delivery and organizational performance. Part of lean product management model category.

## What It Is

Team experimentation empowers development teams to independently work on new ideas, write and change specifications during development, and make changes without external permission — teams decide what to build and test whether it achieves the outcome.

## Problem Solved

- Developers are order-takers, executing requirements without understanding the problem
- Innovation can't happen because every change requires external approval
- Teams can't iterate on solutions because specs are locked
- The best ideas never surface because there's no mechanism for exploration

## Key Practices

1. **Empower teams to work on new ideas** in pursuit of business goals that solve important problems.
2. **Provide teams with information and context** so they can make informed decisions about the right work.
3. **Allow teams to change stories, specifications, and technologies** when they decide it's appropriate.
4. **Hold regular hackathons** for experimentation and sharing ideas.
5. **Allow developers and operators to talk to and observe customers directly.**
6. **Start from the business outcome or problem** — not from predetermined requirements.

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Why It Fails | What To Do Instead |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Treating technical staff as order-takers | Disengagement; no ownership of outcomes | Start from business outcomes; let teams decide what to build |
| No time or mechanism for experimentation | Innovation stagnates | Hackathons, exploration time, experimentation budget |

## How to Measure

- Survey: Teams can work on new ideas without permission
- Teams can change stories and specs during development without asking
- Track permission requests for changing specs — should decrease over time

## Coaching Patterns

1. **Start stories with outcomes, not solutions** — "reduce checkout abandonment by 20%" not "add a popup."
2. **Hold a quarterly hackathon** — 24 hours, any idea, judging by outcomes.
3. **Let developers observe customers** — shadow user testing sessions or support calls.
